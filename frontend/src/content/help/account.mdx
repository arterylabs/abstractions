# Account & Security

Your account settings cover your personal profile, password, and linked OAuth providers. These settings are per-user and don't affect other members of your workspace.

## Profile

Go to **Account** to update:

1. **Name** — your display name across the workspace
2. **Avatar** — upload a profile photo

Your email address is shown on this page but can't be changed here. Contact your workspace owner if you need to update the email on your account.

## Security

Go to **Account → Security** to manage authentication settings.

### Changing your password

1. Enter your current password
2. Enter a new password (minimum 8 characters)
3. Confirm the new password
4. Click **Update password**

If you signed up with OAuth and don't have a password set, this section will prompt you to set one before you can change it.

### Linked OAuth providers

You can link your Google or GitHub account to your Abstractions account. Once linked, you can sign in with either provider in addition to your email and password.

To link a provider:

1. Go to **Account → Security**
2. Click **Link Google** or **Link GitHub**
3. Authorise the OAuth flow

To unlink a provider, click **Unlink** next to the connected provider.

<Callout type="warning">
  You need to have either a password or at least one other provider linked
  before you can unlink an OAuth provider. You cannot be locked out of your
  account this way.
</Callout>

## Advanced settings

Go to **Account → Advanced** to access account-level danger zone actions, including permanently deleting your account.

<Callout type="danger">
  Deleting your account removes your personal data but does not delete the
  workspace or its data. If you're the workspace owner, transfer ownership
  before deleting your account.
</Callout>
